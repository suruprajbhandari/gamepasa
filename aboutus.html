About GamePasa

GamePasa is a gaming platform dedicated to bringing the best gaming experience to Nepali gamers. As our name "GamePasa" suggests, we are your gaming pasa. Pasa means Friend in Newari language. So, we are your friend for gaming.

Our Mission

To promote Nepali🇳🇵 Gaming and provide gamers with the best gaming experience through our World class platform.
